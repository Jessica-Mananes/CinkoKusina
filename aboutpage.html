Five Women. One Kitchen. Endless Filipino Flavors.

Cinko Kusina is a modern Filipino restaurant founded by five passionate women who share a love for authentic Filipino flavors. Every dish we serve reflects our dedication to preserving the taste of home while adding a fresh and creative twist.

Modern Filipino Comfort Food

Our mission is to bring the warmth of Filipino cooking into a modern dining experience. Whether it is a reimagined classic or a new dish inspired by Filipino culture, every plate is made with heart, tradition, and creativity.

At Cinko Kusina, we believe food should be comforting, flavorful, and memorable. We take pride in serving dishes that bring joy, connection, and a true taste of home.
